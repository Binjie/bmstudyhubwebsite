Science

Understand scientific ideas through observation, evidence, and clear explanations.

Science lessons help students connect school topics with real observations, diagrams, data, and everyday examples. The aim is to move beyond memorising facts and build the ability to explain what is happening and why.

Best For

  • Students who find science vocabulary or explanations difficult.
  • Students who need help with investigations, variables, graphs, and evidence.
  • Students preparing for school assessments in physics, chemistry, biology, or earth science topics.

Focus Areas

  • Scientific investigation, fair testing, variables, data, and graph interpretation.
  • Forces, energy, electricity, waves, matter, chemical change, cells, ecosystems, earth, and space.
  • Using evidence to write clearer scientific explanations.

Class Style

Lessons use questions, models, diagrams, simple investigations, and exam-style explanation practice. Students are encouraged to describe, compare, predict, and justify their ideas in their own words.
