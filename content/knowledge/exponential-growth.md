## The Core Idea

Linear growth adds the same amount each step. Exponential growth multiplies by the same factor each step.

That difference looks small at first, but after many steps the gap becomes enormous.

## Life Example

If something improves by 1% each day, it does not simply improve by 365% after a year. Each day builds on the previous day's result, so the change compounds.

This is why habits, interest, population growth, and online sharing can feel slow at first and then suddenly accelerate.

## Classroom Example

Compare these two plans:

- add 2 points every week
- multiply by 1.1 every week

Ask students to draw both curves for 20 weeks. The picture makes the idea much clearer than the formula alone.
