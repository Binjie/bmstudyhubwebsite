## The Core Idea

A vector carries two pieces of information: direction and size.

That simple idea appears in geometry, physics, computer graphics, and data science.

## Life Example

Navigation gives a concrete picture. The path from your current location to your destination can be described as a vector: it points somewhere and has a distance.

Wind, force, velocity, and screen movement work the same way. They are not just numbers; they point.

## Classroom Example

Ask students to move on a grid:

- 3 steps right and 2 steps up
- 1 step left and 4 steps up

Then combine those movements. This makes vector addition visible before introducing formal notation.
