## How to Play

Dual N-Back asks you to compare the **current round** with the round that appeared **N steps earlier**.

For example, in **2-back**, compare the current round with the one from two rounds ago:

| Round | Position | Letter | What to compare |
|-------|----------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Top left | C | Build history |
| 2 | Center | F | Build history |
| 3 | Top left | B | Compare with round 1: position matches |
| 4 | Bottom right | F | Compare with round 2: letter matches |

Each round has two things to track:

- **Position** - where the square appears on the 3x3 grid.
- **Letter** - the spoken and displayed letter.

Press the matching option before the next round starts:

- **F** or **Left Arrow** if the position matches.
- **J** or **Right Arrow** if the letter matches.
- **Space** if both match.
- Do nothing if neither matches.

The first N rounds are just warm-up because there is not enough history to compare yet.

---

## Best Starting Point

Start with **1-back** if the rules are new. Move to **2-back** once the task feels clear. Higher levels are mainly for short, focused challenges.

---

## Practice Tips

- Say the position pattern quietly in your head, such as "top, middle, bottom".
- Keep listening for the letter even when the position feels obvious.
- Prioritize accuracy first; speed becomes easier once the rule is automatic.
- Stop after a few minutes if attention drops. This game should feel focused, not frustrating.
