Attention

Focus and Reaction Check

A light check-in for attention, reaction speed, and consistency across repeated rounds.

Updated
June 2026
Time
5-8 minutes
Audience
All learners

What It Tracks

This check-in records:

  • best response time
  • average response time
  • false starts or missed signals
  • consistency across several rounds

Sample Activity

Students complete five short reaction rounds. After each round, they record the result and briefly note what affected their focus.

Example notes: “I clicked too early,” “I waited more calmly,” or “noise made this round harder.”

How To Use Results

Look for patterns over time. A single low score may only show tiredness. A monthly record can reveal whether sleep, stress, task type, or practice habits affect attention.

How To Use

Treat the result as a conversation starter.

A test can reveal useful patterns, but it should not become a fixed label. Compare results with classroom observation, reflection, and student voice.
