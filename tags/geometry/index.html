Cube Net Builder

June 1, 2026

How to Play

Look at the colored cube. Choose the 2D net that could fold into that same cube.

The colors must land on the correct opposite and neighboring faces. Some options use the same colors but put one face in the wrong place.

Controls

  • Click a net to answer.
  • Drag the cube to rotate it and inspect hidden faces.
  • Press 1-4 to choose an option.
  • Press N or Space for the next question.
  • Press R to reset your score.

Practice Tips

  • First find opposite faces: top-bottom, front-back, left-right.
  • Then check which colors touch each other along an edge.
  • Rotate the cube view before answering if you need another angle.

This game supports geometry learning by connecting flat diagrams with folded 3D objects.

Mental Rotation 3D

June 1, 2026

How to Play

Look at the 3D block shape on the left. Then choose which option shows the same shape after rotation.

The correct answer may be turned around in space, but the block structure is unchanged. Distractors may be mirrored, missing a block, or have one block moved.

Controls

  • Click an option to answer.
  • Press 1-4 to choose an option.
  • Press N or Space for the next question.
  • Press R to reset your score.

Practice Tips

  • Focus on corners, branches, and how many blocks touch each block.
  • Try mentally turning the whole object before comparing details.
  • Watch for mirror images. They often look almost right but cannot be rotated into the target.

This game is useful preparation for geometry, diagrams, engineering drawing, and other tasks that ask students to imagine objects turning in 3D.
